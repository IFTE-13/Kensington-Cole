About the Firm

Three decades of courtroom command, founded on principle.

Our Origins

Founded on conviction. Built in courtrooms.

Kensington & Cole LLP was established in 1994 by former Assistant U.S. Attorney Arthur Kensington with a singular focus: elite boutique advocacy with major-firm firepower, delivered to clients who refuse to settle for less than direct senior partner involvement.

In 2002, commercial litigator Elena Rostova-Cole joined as Co-Managing Partner. The firm now serves individuals, executives, and families across criminal defense, civil litigation, and private wealth matters.

Our Principles

Six non-negotiable standards.

Trial Readiness

We prepare every matter for jury trial. That posture creates real negotiation leverage.

Absolute Discretion

Sensitive matters stay out of public dockets through strict privilege protocols.

Direct Partner Oversight

Every motion, deposition, and hearing is directed by a named senior partner — not delegated.

Early Intervention

We engage at the investigative or pre-charge stage, when options are widest and evidence is freshest.

Candid Assessment

We give honest risk evaluations, not false assurances. Clients make decisions with full information.

Pro Bono Commitment

Partners dedicate hours to innocence advocacy and civil liberties work each year.
